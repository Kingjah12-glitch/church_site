Sunday order of service

Our Sunday service runs from 8:45 AM to 12:00 noon. This is how the morning flows.

  2. Opening prayer

  8. The Lord’s Supper
  9. Giving
  10. Announcements
  11. Song
  12. Closing prayer

Who we are

We are a congregation of the Church of Christ in Abakaliki, gathering each week to worship, study the Bible and encourage one another.

Whether you are new to the city or have been looking for a church home, you are welcome to join us.

Come and worship

Join us on Sundays from 8:45 AM, and on Tuesday, Wednesday and Friday evenings for our classes.

Study the Bible with us

Bible study is part of every Sunday service, and our Wednesday Bible class is open to all.

Find us

We would love to see you. Call or message us with any question, or simply walk in on any service day.
